About BankPredict AI

AI-Powered Term Deposit Marketing Prediction System

The Problem

Commercial banks conduct telemarketing campaigns to sell term deposits. However, contacting every customer indiscriminately leads to high telecommunication costs, agent fatigue, and customer dissatisfaction due to irrelevant sales calls.

The AI Solution

BankPredict AI utilizes Machine Learning algorithms trained on historical customer demographics, contact history, and economic indicators to score customer conversion probability, allowing marketing teams to target high-probability leads effectively.

Machine Learning Workflow

Step 1
Data Collection
Bank Marketing dataset containing 41,188 customer records and 20 features.
Step 2
Data Understanding
Exploratory Data Analysis checking dtypes, distributions, and feature correlations.
Step 3
Data Cleaning
Handling nulls, stripping white-spaces, and deduplicating 1,784 redundant records.
Step 4
Outlier Handling
IQR Interquartile Range trimming on continuous columns (age, campaign, pdays, previous, cons.conf.idx).
Step 5
ColumnTransformer Encoding
OneHotEncoding for categorical variables and StandardScaler for continuous variables.
Step 6
Feature Selection & VIF
Variance Inflation Factor analysis identifying multicollinearity across economic indicators.
Step 7
Train-Test Split
Stratified 75% train / 25% test split preserving class ratios.
Step 8
Pipeline Model Training
Scikit-learn Pipeline with Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, and Random Forests.
Step 9 & 10
Evaluation & Django Web App
Calculating Accuracy, ROC-AUC, and serving inference predictions via Django REST API.
