How BankPredict AI Works

From raw customer inputs to actionable AI marketing intelligence

1
Input Customer Information

Enter customer demographics (Age, Job, Education, Marital Status), credit/loan status, contact details, and current economic metrics via our validated single form or batch CSV upload.

2
Unified Scikit-Learn Pipeline Processing

The input data is fed directly into a serialized Scikit-learn ColumnTransformer pipeline that standardizes continuous variables and applies one-hot encoding without any training/prediction mismatch.

3
ML Probability Scoring & XAI Breakdown

The Logistic Regression classifier predicts subscription probability (0% - 100%). Simultaneously, our Explainable AI (XAI) engine extracts coefficient weights to highlight key positive drivers and negative risk factors.

4
MySQL Persistence & Analytics

Prediction results, probabilities, recommendations, and XAI explanations are safely persisted into MySQL, instantly updating your personal prediction history log and dashboard Chart.js analytics.
